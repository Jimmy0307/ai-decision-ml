## Number and format rules

- Numbers stay exactly as in the English version and the log. Use the same decimals and Arabic digits.
- Thousands separators stay as in the English (e.g. 8,512,829).
- Citations use the same `\citet` / `\citep` keys. Author names stay in English.
- Labels: reuse the English labels with a `zh:` prefix (e.g. `\label{zh:sec:study2}`), so the two documents never clash.
- Verdict labels (HOLE_NOT_SUPPORTED, PARTIAL, …) stay in English, in `\texttt{}` with `\allowbreak` after underscores.
