# Preregistration R1: re-analysis of an open-data published case (review protocol §8)

**Status:** frozen 2026-11-19. Frozen before any data file of the case was downloaded or opened by this project. A feasibility subagent opened one two-mode monthly file (`net2m_2009-08-01.txt`) to confirm the format; it computed nothing and recorded no statistic. The commit timestamp is the freeze record. Changes after the freeze are dated amendments below the text; the text itself is not edited.

**Nature:** a confirmatory diagnostic with the rules fixed in advance. The case was chosen because its two-mode data are open and small, not because a particular result was expected. Either outcome is reported. Language about the original study stays neutral: the re-analysis asks what a null model attributes to affiliation structure; it does not grade the original study.

## 1. Case

- **Study:** Seierstad, C., & Opsahl, T. (2011). For the few not the many? The effects of affirmative action on presence, prominence, and social capital of women directors in Norway. *Scandinavian Journal of Management*, 27(1), 44–54.
- **Data:** the authors' public dataset of board memberships of Norwegian public limited companies (http://www.opsahl.co.uk/boardsandgender/data.php):
  - monthly two-mode edge lists (company id, person id), May 2002 to August 2011;
  - matching one-mode files;
  - a person list with gender.
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC 3.0, as stated on the site. It is used for non-commercial research only.

**Step 0 (before any data are downloaded):**
- Record the article's own description of its network measures and the claims it draws from them. Sources are the publicly accessible abstract and any open full text or author page.
- Store the record in `R1_CLAIM_RECORD.md`.
- If the article does not read a centrality measure as brokerage, prominence through bridging, or social-capital advantage, the diagnostics in §3 still run unchanged. They are then reported as tests of the generic brokerage reading of these data, not of the article's claim.

## 2. Snapshots

- **Primary:** the last available monthly file.
- **Secondary:**
  - the first available file;
  - the available file nearest to 2008-02-01 (after the deadline for the quota).
- Each snapshot is a two-mode graph: directors × boards, unweighted, with duplicate rows removed. Directors without a gender in the person list are kept in the network and excluded from gender-based focal sets.
- The one-mode projection links two directors who share at least one board. It is unweighted for betweenness. The edge weight (shared boards) is kept for constraint.

## 3. Diagnostics

**D1 Projection closure (all snapshots; descriptive):**
- **(a)** Transitivity (global clustering) of the one-mode projection.
- **(b)** The share of projection triangles whose three directors share at least one board. These triangles are produced by board cliques, not by separate ties.
- **(c)** The two-mode transitivity of Opsahl (2013), computed on the two-mode graph.
- Reading rule: if (b) ≥ 0.90, projection closure is reported as "dominated by board cliques".

**D2 Constraint identity (primary snapshot; check):**
- On the two-mode graph, Burt constraint is computed for each director, with equal tie weights. Its agreement with 1/degree (number of boards) is reported: maximum absolute difference, and the share of directors with |difference| < 1e-9.
- This is an implementation check of the identity in the manuscript. It is not a finding.

**D3 Brokerage reading against an affiliation-preserving null (primary snapshot confirmatory; secondary snapshots descriptive):**
- **Null:** 1,000 random two-mode graphs (200 for each secondary snapshot).
  - Generated by curveball trades that keep every director's number of boards and every board's size.
  - At least 5 × (number of two-mode edges) trades per replicate.
  - Seed 20261119; replicate r uses the seed sequence [20261119, snapshot index, r].
- **Statistics,** computed on the one-mode projection of the observed graph and of each null graph. Betweenness is normalised and unweighted; constraint follows Burt and uses shared-board weights.
  - **S1:** mean betweenness of women directors minus mean betweenness of men directors.
  - **S2:** median betweenness of multi-seat directors, those holding ≥ 3 boards in the snapshot (any gender).
  - **S3:** median constraint of women directors minus median constraint of men directors.
  - **S4:** median constraint of multi-seat directors.
- **Tests:** one-sided Monte Carlo p-values, p = (1 + #{null ≥ observed}) / (1 + R) for S1 and S2, and the lower tail for S3 and S4.
- **Decision rule, per statistic, primary snapshot only:**
  - `BEYOND_AFFILIATION` if p < .05;
  - otherwise `EXPLAINED_BY_AFFILIATION`. This means the observed value is within what seat counts and board sizes alone produce.
- **Effect size:** observed ÷ null median, with the null 2.5–97.5 percentiles.
- **Overall label for the primary snapshot:**
  - `BROKERAGE_READING_SURVIVES` if at least one of S1–S2 and at least one of S3–S4 is `BEYOND_AFFILIATION`;
  - `BROKERAGE_READING_EXPLAINED` if all four are `EXPLAINED_BY_AFFILIATION`;
  - `MIXED` otherwise.

## 4. Stop rules and deviations

- If the files cannot be retrieved or parsed, the re-analysis stops and is reported as not done, with the reason.
- If curveball mixing is too slow on the available hardware, R may be reduced to 500 for the primary snapshot. This is recorded as an amendment before any null statistic is inspected.
- Any change to snapshots, statistics or rules after data are downloaded is recorded as a dated amendment, and the affected result is labelled exploratory.

## 5. Outputs

- **`R1_RESULTS.json`:** D1 to D3 for all snapshots and the labels.
- **`R1_CLAIM_RECORD.md`:** the step-0 record.
- A short report.
- The raw data are not committed (licence), only the download URLs, file names and SHA-256 hashes.
- The manuscript reports the result in the review section and in limitations, whatever the result.
